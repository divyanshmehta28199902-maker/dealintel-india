Add AI Deal Preview Before Publishing

IMPORTANT

Do NOT redesign the application.

⸻

New Final Step

After the user clicks Next on the Review page, show a new screen called:

AI Deal Preview

This page summarizes the deal and helps the user improve it before publishing.

The analysis should complete in 2–5 seconds with a loading animation.

⸻

Layout

Display the analysis as professional dashboard cards.

⸻

1. Estimated Valuation Range

Estimate a valuation using the available financial data.

If EBITDA is positive:

* EBITDA Multiple method

If EBITDA is negative:

* Revenue Multiple method

Display:

Estimated Enterprise Value

Valuation Range (Low–High)

Method Used

Confidence Level

Explain briefly why that method was chosen.

Never claim the valuation is exact.

⸻

2. Deal Quality Score

Calculate dynamically.

Score from 0–100.

Breakdown:

Business Information

Financial Information

Narrative Quality

Documents

Verification

Display a progress bar.

65/100

Good Listing

90/100

Investor Ready

3. Investor Readiness Score

4. Strengths

Generate bullet points based on the submitted information.

Examples:

• Consistent revenue growth

• Low debt

Improve the Seller Valuation Section (Without Redesigning the UI)

IMPORTANT

Do NOT redesign the Seller Portal.

Do NOT modify navigation.

Do NOT change routing.

Do NOT modify authentication.

Do NOT change subscription permissions.

Do NOT alter existing layouts, branding, colors, typography, spacing, or theme.

Keep the current valuation page exactly as it is visually.

The objective is to make the valuation results significantly more insightful and professional while preserving the existing interface.

⸻

1. Add an AI Valuation Summary

After the valuation is calculated, generate a concise explanation of the result.

The summary should explain:

* Which valuation method contributed the most.
* Why the estimated value is high or low.
* Which financial metrics had the biggest impact.
* Whether positive EBITDA or revenue multiples were used.
* Whether the business appears attractive based only on the submitted financial information.

Keep the explanation concise (4–8 sentences).

Do not fabricate facts or provide investment advice.

⸻

2. Add Valuation Confidence

Display a confidence score (0–100%) based on the completeness and consistency of the submitted information.

Example factors:

* Revenue entered
* EBITDA entered
* Growth rate provided
* Industry selected
* Asking price entered
* Financial consistency
* Required fields completed

Display:

Confidence: 82%

Label:

Low

Moderate

High

Include a short explanation of why the confidence is at that level.

⸻

3. Add Key Drivers

Display the primary factors influencing the valuation.

Examples:

Positive Drivers

* Strong EBITDA margin
* Consistent revenue growth
* Attractive industry multiple
* Healthy profitability

Negative Drivers

* Declining growth
* High leverage
* Low margins
* Missing financial history

Only generate observations supported by the entered data.

⸻

Positive Considerations

Potential Concerns

Examples:

Positive

* Growing industry
* Healthy profitability
* Stable revenue

Potential Concerns

* Limited documentation
* Revenue concentration
* High valuation expectations

Do not make unsupported claims.

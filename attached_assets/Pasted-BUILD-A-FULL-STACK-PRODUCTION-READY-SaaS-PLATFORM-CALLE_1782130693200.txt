🧠 VALUATION ENGINE (REAL LOGIC)

MODE 1: BASIC

EV Calculation:

* EV = EBITDA × industry_multiple
* Store multiples in DB

Simple DCF:

* 5-year projection
* Discount rate (10–18%)

⸻

MODE 2: ADVANCED DCF

Inputs:

* Revenue growth rate
* EBITDA margin
* CapEx
* Working capital
* Discount rate

Process:

1. Forecast revenue (5 years)
2. Calculate EBITDA
3. Compute Free Cash Flow:
    FCF = EBITDA - CapEx - Change in WC
4. Discount FCF
5. Calculate Terminal Value:
    TV = FCF × (1 + g) / (r - g)
6. Discount TV
7. Final DCF valuation

⸻
